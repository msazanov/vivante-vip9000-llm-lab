## Evidence boundary

The enabled unit and matching target hashes establish persistent filesystem
configuration and a successful live start. A cold-boot reapplication remains
unverified until a separately approved reboot. The 30 °C setting makes the
fan run at the top of the existing curve at the observed operating
temperatures; it does not force PWM independently of the kernel governor and
does not replace the 85 °C benchmark abort ceiling.
